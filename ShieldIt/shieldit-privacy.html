1. Institutional Context & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the operation of ShieldIt (the "Extension"), a dedicated browser proctoring and assessment environment enforcement tool developed for the Institute of Aeronautical Engineering (IARE), Dundigal, Hyderabad, India.

ShieldIt is designed exclusively to preserve academic honesty during formal continuous internal evaluations (CIE), semester-end examinations (SEE), laboratory coding challenges, and recruitment assessments hosted on the institutional BuildIt platform (https://buildit.iare.ac.in).

ShieldIt strictly adheres to the principle of data minimization and operates with complete transparency. It operates solely when an active examination session is initiated by a verified student and detaches automatically upon exam submission.

2. Browser Permissions & Purpose Specification

To satisfy Google Chrome Web Store policies, every requested browser permission is strictly necessary and directly restricted to assessment integrity functions:

Permission Functional Purpose Privacy Protection & Safeguard
management Temporarily disables active third-party extensions (such as external AI chatbots, solvers, automated copilot tools, and translation overlays) during the active duration of an exam. Captures a temporary local array of extension IDs solely inside local memory. All paused extensions are automatically re-enabled immediately when the exam is submitted or completed. No extension names or IDs are transmitted to external servers.
tabs Verifies that the candidate maintains focus on the active examination tab, detecting window minimization or switching away to other tabs during active testing. Zero browsing history access. ShieldIt never records, reads, or transmits the URLs, titles, or page content of any candidate tabs. It strictly inspects tab focus change events.
system.display Detects connected monitor count to verify compliance with institutional single-display exam guidelines and detect unauthorized secondary monitors or HDMI splitters. Only queries the numeric count of active displays. No hardware identifiers, serial numbers, screen contents, or monitor models are collected or stored.
storage Retains the student's pre-exam extension state locally in chrome.storage.local so extensions can be reliably restored even if the browser is closed or refreshed mid-session. Stored entirely within the candidate's local browser storage. The data is wiped clean once the exam session concludes and extensions have been restored.

3. Data Collection, Transmission, & Storage

ShieldIt enforces a strict zero personal surveillance policy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): ShieldIt does not request, log, or store student names, roll numbers, credentials, email addresses, or contact information.
  • No Keystroke Logging: Key interception is restricted solely to preventing unauthorized operating system shortcuts (e.g., F12, Ctrl+Shift+I, Alt+Tab). Keystrokes typed into code editors or problem inputs are never recorded or logged by the extension.
  • No Media Capturing: ShieldIt contains no camera, microphone, or continuous screen-recording software.
  • No Third-Party Trackers or Analytics: The extension codebase contains zero analytics packages, telemetry beacons, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No Data Monetization: Neither IARE nor BuildIt sells, leases, or trades candidate usage data to commercial entities under any circumstances.

4. Automatic Restoration of Extensions

The Institute of Aeronautical Engineering recognizes that candidates maintain personalized extensions for their regular academic and personal browsing. ShieldIt is programmed to respect user configurations:

  • Upon normal submission of an exam or navigation to the exam results page, ShieldIt immediately triggers a restoration routine that re-enables all previously active extensions.
  • If an examination is ended abruptly due to a computer restart or power outage, ShieldIt's popup interface provides a one-click "Restore My Extensions" utility so students can restore their environment without administrative intervention.

5. Cryptographic Session Scoping

Communication between the institutional BuildIt web application and the ShieldIt extension occurs over an isolated cryptographic message bridge using ephemeral session tokens generated at exam start. This architectural boundary guarantees that:

  1. Unauthenticated or third-party web domains cannot invoke ShieldIt commands or manipulate extension states.
  2. Lockdown guards are active exclusively on verified examination URLs (e.g., https://buildit.iare.ac.in/exams/*/session).
  3. Normal browsing outside scheduled examination windows remains entirely unaffected.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

⚠ Compliance with Google Limited Use Requirements

ShieldIt's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information retrieved from browser APIs is used strictly to provide the exam lockdown service and is never repurposed for profiling, advertising, or external syndication.
